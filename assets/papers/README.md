# Hosted APS journal PDFs

These files are unmodified final journal PDFs of articles coauthored by Jiajun Li.
APS permits free hosting on an author's own website with bibliographic attribution
and the applicable copyright notice:
https://journals.aps.org/copyrightFAQ.html

The PRX and Physical Review Research papers carry CC BY 4.0 licenses; their
original license notices are retained. Other files retain APS copyright notices.
The publication pages display the applicable notice alongside their citations.
These PDFs are not covered by any license that may apply to the website code.

`data/hosted-pdfs.json` records download sources, SHA-256 checksums, and remaining
papers. Downloaded on 25 October 2026 from APS and public institutional repositories.
First-page journal headers, DOIs, author names, and rights notices were checked.

## Journal copies still needed

Publisher downloads required access, or no accessible final journal copy was
found. Their existing arXiv links remain in place. To add a supplied journal PDF,
verify its DOI, preserve the original bytes, record its source and checksum in the
manifest, and set the bibliography's `pdf`, `pdf_source`, and `pdf_copyright`
fields (and `pdf_license` for a Creative Commons paper). Then rebuild the site.
