01 / PROFILE

From machine learning
to real-time decisions.

Since 2022, I have worked at IMC Trading and Qube Research & Technologies on high-frequency trading and short-term forecasting. My work includes machine-learning models for market-making strategies, market microstructure research, and the implementation of trading systems and low-latency inference in C++.

I have spent more than a decade developing computational methods and scientific software, mainly in C++, Python, Fortran, and CUDA. Much of this work involves numerical algorithms and performance-critical code.

Trained as a theoretical physicist, I combine mathematical insight with a hands-on, empirical approach to time series research, trading strategy design, and performance engineering. My work at QRT and IMC has deepened my understanding of statistics and market microstructure.
