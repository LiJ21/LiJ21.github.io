From machine learning to real-time decisions.

I’m a quantitative researcher at QRT in Zürich, working on high-frequency trading and short-term forecasting. Since 2022, my work at QRT and IMC has spanned machine-learning signals, market microstructure research, and real-time trading systems.

I combine statistical research with hands-on performance engineering. For more than a decade, I’ve developed high-performance software in C++, Python, Fortran, and CUDA. My current open-source work focuses on low-latency inference, bounded-memory model training, and efficient C++ data structures.
