AI, computation, and trading.

Quant Researcher at QRT

I develop algorithms and computational methods across quantitative finance, AI, and quantum physics. My current work applies machine learning and high-performance computing to high-frequency trading, market microstructure research, and short-term forecasting. Previously, I studied strongly correlated quantum systems and nonequilibrium light–matter dynamics.

For more than a decade, I’ve built high-performance software in C++, Python, Fortran, and CUDA. My open-source work includes quantum simulation tools, low-latency AI inference, and efficient C++ data structures.
